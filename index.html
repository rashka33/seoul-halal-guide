How to read the tiers

Near me asks for your location only when you tap. It stays on this page and is never saved. The map then loads OpenStreetMap tiles for your area.

  • Mosque
  • Restaurant
  • Grocery
  • Prayer room
  • Café

Distances are straight-line estimates, measured from the mosque unless you use Near me. Walking minutes assume 5 km/h; hills and routes take longer. Use Naver for walking directions.
